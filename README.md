# case-study

## Applied Financial Math for Decision-Making Project

In this project, you assume the role of a **Senior Investment Advisor** at **Dynamic Wealth Management (DWM)**—a prestigious firm managing over **$2 billion in assets** for high-net-worth individuals and institutions.

Headquartered in the financial hub of **New York City**, DWM is renowned for its personalized service and innovative investment strategies.

You oversee a significant client portfolio, leveraging your financial knowledge to craft tailored financial plans and deliver strategic investment recommendations aligned with each client’s unique objectives.

A key part of your role is staying ahead of market dynamics, global economic shifts, and developments in financial math. You analyze complex financial data, track the performance of various asset classes, and stay informed about industry trends. Your insights and forecasts directly shape the strategic decisions you present to clients.
