Case 4

You meet with Andrew Thompson, who expressed interest in diversifying his portfolio by acquiring fixed-income securities. Andrew presents the following securities.

Bond A – $1,000 face value, 6% annual coupon rate, four-year maturity

Bond B – $1,000 face value, 4% semi-annual coupon rate, three-year maturity

Bond C – $1,000 face value, 8% quarterly coupon rate, two-year maturity

Your task is to analyze these bonds and determine which offers the highest current yield.

Once you've completed your analysis, present your findings. Which bonds (A, B, or C) offer the highest current yield? What is the price of each bond? Justify your answer with appropriate calculations and reasoning.

Tip 1: Assume that the market discount rate is 7%.

Tip 2: Use Excel’s Case 4 sheet to perform your calculations. 

To obtain the current yield for each of the securities, take the following steps.

1. Calculate the market price for each of the three bonds using the following formula:

\[PV=\frac{PMT}{{(1+r)}^1}+\frac{PMT}{{(1+r)}^2}+...+\frac{PMT+FV}{{(1+r)}^N}\]

Alternatively, you can use Excel’s PV function to obtain the same result. 

2. Divide the annual coupon rate of each bond by its market price:

\[Current\ Yield=\frac{Annual\ Coupon\ Payment}{Current\ Market\ Price}\]

Case 5

Lisa Moore has heard about the potentially attractive rates of return offered by money market instruments. She seeks your expertise to evaluate the three following potential investments.

Investment 1 – \$1,000 face value, \$960 market price, 90-day maturity tenure

Investment 2 – \$1,000 face value, \$950 market price, 140-day maturity tenure

Investment 3 – \$1,000 face value, \$940 market price, 120-day maturity tenure

Your task is to calculate the Holding Period Yield (HPY), the Effective Annual Yield (EAY), and the Money Market Yield (MNY) for each of the three investments. Which of the three bonds should Lisa go for? Once you've completed your calculations, present your findings.

 

To obtain the Holding Period Yield (HPY), the Effective Annual Yield (EAY), and the Money Market Yield (MNY) for each of the three investments, take the following steps.

1. Calculate the holding period return for each of the three bonds using the following formula:

\[Holding\ period\ yield\ (HPY)=\frac{P_1-P_0+D_1}{P_0}\]

2. Acquire the effective annual yield using the HPY from Step 1:

\[EAY=\left(1+HPY\right)^\frac{365}{t}-1\]

3. Estimate the money market yield using the following formula:

\[MMY=HPY\times\left(\frac{360}{t}\right)\]
